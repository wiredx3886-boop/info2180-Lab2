Who is JP St. Mary?

October 03, 2026 by Josiah Smith

JP St. Mary’s has a range of tropical snacks that are sold locally and internationally. Their line of tropical snacks includes banana, plantain, cassava and breadfruit chips which are manufactured at in factories located in you guessed it St. Mary, Jamaica and Monte Cristi, Dominican Republic.

Their fresh produce line of products includes ripe bananas, green bananas, pineapples and coconuts which are grown and harvested on our farm in St. Mary, Jamaica.

What Are Banana Chips?

October 10, 2026 by Josiah Smith

A banana chip (sometimes called banana crisp -> bloody english twats) is a deep-fried or dried, generally crispy slice of banana. It is usually made from firmer, starchier banana varieties (cooking bananas or plantains) like the saba and Nendran cultivars. It can be sweet or savory and can be covered with sugar, honey, salt, or various spices

Fried banana chips are usually produced from under-ripe banana slices deep-fried in sunflower oil or coconut oil. These chips are dry (like potato chips), contain about 4% water (table), and can be salted, spiced, sugar-coated, or jaggery-coated.
